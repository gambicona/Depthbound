Race / Species Balance Review
Updated: 2026-05-09

Purpose
This note reviews the current race/species and subrace implementation for game balance in this dungeon crawler. It is not a rules transcript. It is a practical tuning pass for this game's current systems: grid combat, movement, damage, inventory, resting, and d20 checks.

Current Important Context
- There is no light system, so darkvision has no value.
- There is no spell system yet, so innate spellcasting has no active value.
- There is no social/tool system yet, so languages, tools, and social traits have little or no value.
- Weapon and armor proficiencies are stored/displayed but not enforced yet.
- Ability score bonuses, speed, damage resistance, Dwarven Toughness, Halfling Lucky, Half-Orc Relentless Endurance, and Half-Orc Savage Attacks are active.
- Because many noncombat traits are intentionally inactive, some races currently feel much weaker than their tabletop versions.


Balance Snapshot

Strong Right Now

Human / Standard Human
- +1 to all six stats is very strong in a game where most checks and attacks are direct ability/modifier math.
- It is especially good for generalist heroes and for players who do not want to optimize.
- No special combat trait, but the raw stat total is high.

Hill Dwarf
- +2 CON, +1 WIS, poison resistance, 25 ft speed, and +1 HP per level.
- Very durable.
- The lower speed matters, but in tight dungeon rooms it may not fully offset the durability.
- Strong defensive pick.

Half-Orc
- +2 STR and +1 CON already fit fighter-style combat very well.
- Relentless Endurance is extremely valuable because it prevents a downed-state spiral.
- Savage Attacks is a clean offensive boost.
- Strongest melee damage/survival package.

Halfling / Stout Halfling
- +2 DEX and Lucky are very good because Lucky touches almost every important d20 roll.
- Stout adds CON and poison resistance, making it a very strong defensive dex option.
- 25 ft speed is a real cost but not always painful.

Dragonborn
- +2 STR, +1 CHA, 30 ft speed, and resistance from ancestry.
- Solid, but not as explosive as Half-Orc because breath weapon is not implemented.
- Strong when the chosen resistance matches dungeon damage types.


Medium Right Now

Mountain Dwarf
- +2 CON and +2 STR are numerically huge.
- Poison resistance is useful.
- 25 ft speed is the main drawback.
- Light/medium armor proficiency is stored, but because proficiency is not enforced yet, this part has little practical value.
- Strong stats, but not as interesting as Hill Dwarf or Half-Orc until proficiency matters.

Wood Elf
- +2 DEX, +1 WIS, and 35 ft speed are all active.
- Fast and reliable, especially for ranged or mobile heroes.
- Missing stealth/darkvision/trance means it is mostly a movement-stat package.

Drow / High Elf / Eladrin
- +2 DEX plus +1 CHA or INT.
- Weapon proficiencies are stored but not enforced.
- Innate spells are stored but inactive.
- Good stat line, but currently less exciting than Wood Elf.

Tiefling Variants
- Fire resistance is active and useful.
- Ability bonuses vary by variant.
- Innate spells are inactive, so many variants are mostly stat package + fire resistance.
- Zariel is currently the best fighter-facing Tiefling because +1 STR helps directly.


Weak Right Now

Gnome
- +2 INT is weak for a mostly fighter-like combat game unless INT checks/trap interactions become central.
- 25 ft speed hurts.
- Gnome Cunning is stored but inactive.
- Forest Gnome and Deep Gnome get DEX, which helps. Rock Gnome gets CON, which helps a bit.
- Still underpowered compared with Halfling or Dwarf.

Half-Elf
- +2 CHA and two flexible +1s are good, but not enough to replace inactive Fey Ancestry, skills, and spell/variant features.
- Flexible stats help, but it has no active combat trait right now.
- Needs either active skill value or a small dungeon-facing feature.

Ghostwise / Lightfoot Halfling
- Lucky keeps all Halflings relevant.
- Ghostwise and Lightfoot lose value because their subrace traits are social/stealth oriented and inactive.
- Stout is clearly the best Halfling right now.

Non-Zariel Tieflings
- Fire resistance helps, but spell traits are inactive.
- Variants with +INT or +WIS can feel weaker for fighter builds unless the player values checks.


Main Balance Problem
The current implementation favors races with active combat math:
- Direct ability bonuses
- Damage resistance
- Extra HP
- Once-per-rest survival
- Extra crit damage
- Speed

It undervalues races whose tabletop identity depends on:
- Spells
- Darkvision
- Social features
- Tool proficiencies
- Stealth rules
- Save advantage against specific magical effects

That means the current playable balance is not yet "fair" if judged by the full tabletop race package. It is reasonable for a first implementation, but a later pass should give every race at least one active dungeon-combat or exploration hook.


Suggested Balanced Feature Budget
For this game, each race/subrace should aim for roughly:
- One primary stat package.
- One always-relevant dungeon feature.
- One situational feature.
- One drawback only if the active features are especially strong.

Approximate value guide:
- +2/+1 ability bonuses: baseline.
- Flexible +1/+1: valuable but not enough alone.
- Resistance: medium value, high in themed dungeons.
- 35 ft speed: medium value.
- 25 ft speed: meaningful drawback.
- +1 HP per level: high value.
- Relentless Endurance: high value.
- Halfling Lucky: high value.
- Extra crit die: medium value.
- Weapon/armor proficiency: currently low value until enforced.
- Stored spells/darkvision/social/tool traits: currently zero active value.


Suggested Balanced Active Set

Human
Keep:
- +1 to all stats.
- 30 ft speed.

Optional balance idea:
- No extra features needed.
- Human is simple and strong enough.


Dwarf Base
Keep:
- +2 CON.
- 25 ft speed.
- Poison resistance.

Suggested future active feature:
- Add "Dwarven Stability": once per turn, if moved by a monster effect, reduce forced movement by 5 ft.
- This gives dwarf identity without needing tools/stonecunning.

Hill Dwarf
Keep:
- +1 WIS.
- +1 HP per level.

Balance note:
- Strong but fine because speed is 25 ft.

Mountain Dwarf
Keep:
- +2 STR.
- Armor proficiencies stored.

Suggested balance idea:
- Once armor proficiency is enforced, Mountain Dwarf becomes good naturally.
- Until then, consider adding "Armor Comfort": ignore 5 ft of movement penalty from heavy armor or armor slowdown effects if such effects exist later.

Duergar
Current issue:
- +1 STR alone does not make up for inactive Duergar magic/darkvision.

Suggested active feature:
- "Duergar Endurance": once per long rest, gain resistance to one hit of poison or necrotic damage after damage is rolled.
- Or "Enlarge Placeholder": once per long rest, add +1d4 melee damage for one turn.


Elf Base
Keep:
- +2 DEX.
- 30 ft speed.
- Perception proficiency stored.

Suggested future active feature:
- "Elven Focus": once per short rest, reroll a failed trap spotting/investigation check.
- This gives elves a dungeon-relevant identity without darkvision.

Drow
Current issue:
- Drow spells/darkvision are inactive.

Suggested active feature:
- "Drow Ambush": once per combat, if attacking a monster that has not acted this round, add +1 to hit.
- Avoid sunlight sensitivity unless there is a light/surface system.

High Elf
Current issue:
- Cantrip inactive.

Suggested active feature:
- "Arcane Training": +1 to disarm/investigate magical furniture/traps, if trap types later support magical tags.
- Short-term alternative: +1 INT checks involving dungeon objects.

Eladrin
Current issue:
- Fey Step inactive.

Suggested active feature:
- "Fey Step Placeholder": once per long rest, move up to 10 ft without triggering opportunity attacks.
- This is easy to balance and fits the tactical grid.

Shadar-kai
Keep:
- +1 CON.
- Necrotic resistance.

Suggested active feature:
- If teleport gets implemented, give a short teleport plus resistance for one turn.
- Until then, necrotic resistance + CON is acceptable but situational.

Wood Elf
Keep:
- +1 WIS.
- 35 ft speed.

Balance note:
- This is already a clean, useful package.
- No immediate buff needed.


Dragonborn
Keep:
- +2 STR.
- +1 CHA.
- 30 ft speed.
- Ancestry resistance.

Current issue:
- Breath weapon is inactive, so Dragonborn loses its signature active tool.

Suggested active feature:
- Add Breath Weapon as a once-per-short-rest cone/line action.
- Damage should scale gently by dungeon category or hero level.
- Suggested starting version:
  - Action, 15 ft cone or 20 ft line.
  - Save DC = 8 + proficiency + CON mod.
  - Damage = 2d6 at low levels, half on save.
  - Damage type from ancestry.

Chromatic
- With breath implemented, add no further buff initially.

Gem
- If flight/telepathy stay inactive, let Gem choose from rarer resistances as its advantage.
- Force/psychic/radiant/thunder/necro resistance can be strong depending on dungeon type.

Metallic
- Later add a defensive/control breath option, but start with the same damage breath for balance simplicity.


Gnome Base
Current issue:
- +2 INT and 25 ft speed are weak in current combat.

Suggested active feature:
- "Gnome Cunning": once per short rest, reroll a failed INT/WIS/CHA saving throw.
- Since save context is limited, apply it only to non-physical saves if the game can identify ability.
- This would make gnomes feel clever and defensively unique.

Deep Gnome
- +1 DEX helps.
- Suggested active feature: +1 to disarm/spot traps while underground/cave themes, or always +1 to trap spotting.

Forest Gnome
- +1 DEX helps.
- Suggested active feature: "Minor Illusion Placeholder": once per combat, impose disadvantage on the next attack against the gnome.

Rock Gnome
- +1 CON helps.
- Suggested active feature: "Tinker Patch": once per long rest, restore 1 spent charge to a nonmagical consumable/tool item, or gain +1 to disarm traps.


Half-Elf
Keep:
- +2 CHA.
- Two flexible +1 bonuses.
- 30 ft speed.

Current issue:
- Lacks active identity while skills/spells are inactive.

Suggested active feature:
- "Versatile Training": choose one of three active packages during creation:
  - Scout: +1 to trap spotting/investigation.
  - Duelist: +1 initiative.
  - Support: +1 to Medicine checks.
- This makes Half-Elf flexible without being strictly stronger than specialists.

Drow / High / Wood Half-Elf
- Drow: could later inherit Drow Ambush.
- High: could later inherit Arcane Training.
- Wood: could later choose 35 ft speed OR stealth feature.


Halfling Base
Keep:
- +2 DEX.
- 25 ft speed.
- Lucky.

Balance note:
- Lucky is very strong. The speed drawback is important and should remain.

Ghostwise
Current issue:
- +1 WIS is okay, but Silent Speech is inactive.

Suggested active feature:
- "Quiet Coordination": once per combat, switching target with Tab also gives +1 to the next attack against that target by this halfling, or allow one adjacent ally to ignore opportunity attack from one monster this turn.

Lightfoot
Current issue:
- +1 CHA is weak for current combat.

Suggested active feature:
- "Naturally Stealthy Placeholder": once per combat, if adjacent to an ally, impose disadvantage on one ranged attack against the Lightfoot.

Stout
Keep:
- +1 CON.
- Poison resistance.

Balance note:
- Strongest Halfling currently, but acceptable because poison is situational and speed is 25 ft.


Half-Orc
Keep:
- +2 STR.
- +1 CON.
- 30 ft speed.
- Relentless Endurance.
- Savage Attacks.

Balance note:
- Very strong for melee.
- This is okay if other races get their missing active features later.
- If it proves too strong now, tune Relentless Endurance to once per dungeon instead of once per long rest, but that may feel harsh.


Tiefling Base
Keep:
- Fire resistance.
- 30 ft speed.

Current issue:
- Many variants rely on spells that are inactive.

Suggested active feature:
- Give every Tiefling "Infernal Spark": once per short rest, after hitting with an attack, add 1d4 fire damage.
- This gives them an active identity without implementing full spellcasting.
- It should not stack too high with future smite/spell features.

Variant ideas:
- Baalzebul: poison/decay theme, once per rest add 1d4 poison instead of fire.
- Dispater: defensive trick, once per rest +2 AC against one attack.
- Fierna: charm theme, once per rest one monster has -1 to attack this hero for one turn.
- Glasya: stealth trick, once per rest Disengage as bonus action.
- Levistus: cold defense, once per rest gain temporary HP equal to level + CON mod.
- Mammon: utility, +1 to loot/chest investigation or trap disarm.
- Mephistopheles: fire offense, Infernal Spark upgrades to 1d6.
- Zariel: martial smite, once per rest add 1d4 radiant/fire on melee hit.


Recommended Next Implementation Order
1. Dragonborn Breath Weapon.
2. Gnome Cunning or trap-focused gnome feature.
3. Half-Elf flexible active package.
4. Tiefling Infernal Spark variants.
5. Eladrin/Shadar-kai/Drow tactical once-per-rest features.
6. Proficiency enforcement for weapons and armor.
7. Condition-aware saving throws, then activate Fey Ancestry, Brave, Gnome Cunning in a more faithful way.
8. Spell system, then migrate stored racial spell traits into actual usable abilities.


Suggested Balanced Baseline If You Want Simple Fairness Now
If the goal is good game balance before a spell system exists, every race should have:
- Ability score bonuses.
- Speed.
- At least one active combat/exploration feature.

Minimal additions to reach that:
- Dwarf: already okay.
- Elf: add one trap/perception reroll per short rest.
- Dragonborn: add breath weapon.
- Gnome: add one mental save/check reroll per short rest.
- Half-Elf: add flexible Scout/Duelist/Support package.
- Halfling: already okay.
- Half-Orc: already okay.
- Tiefling: add Infernal Spark once per short rest.
- Human: already okay.

This would make the list feel much more even in the current dungeon crawler while leaving room for full spell/social systems later.
