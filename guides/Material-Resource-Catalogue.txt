Material Resource Catalogue

This catalogue is sorted the same way the Material Satchel renders in game:
category alphabetically, then material name alphabetically.

Random fetch quest means the rotating merchant/smith Material Commission system:
- Exact: the commission names this item directly.
- Flexible: the commission accepts this item because it matches a category or tag requirement.
- No: currently not requested by the rotating commission table.

Fixed quest use is listed separately when the item is part of a named non-random quest chain.
