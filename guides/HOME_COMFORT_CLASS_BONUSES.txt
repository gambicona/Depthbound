Home Comfort Class Bonuses

These bonuses are granted when an active party hero leaves home for a dungeon.
They last only for that dungeon run and are removed when the party returns home.

Universal comfort bonuses:
1 comfort point: temporary HP equal to 1% of max HP.
5 comfort points: temporary HP equal to 5% of max HP.
10 comfort points: +1 hit die available for this dungeon run.
20 comfort points: temporary HP equal to 10% of max HP.
60 comfort points: temporary HP equal to 20% of max HP.

Removed from the universal track for balance:
+1 to all skill checks.
+1 to all saving throws.
Universal Second Wind.

Class comfort bonuses:

Barbarian

80 comfort points: +1 additional Rage use.
