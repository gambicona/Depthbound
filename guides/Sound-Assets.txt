Sound asset wiring
==================

Put sound files in these folders relative to index.html:

Background music, MP3, looped automatically:
- assets/sounds/music/home.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/oldGuardroom-exploration.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/oldGuardroom-combat.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/oldGuardroom-boss-combat.mp3

Village faction screen music, MP3, looped while that faction board is open:
- assets/sounds/music/village-trophy-lodge.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/village-gravebinders.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/village-gravebinders-2.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/village-crucible-collegium.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/village-crucible-collegium-2.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/village-antiquarian-society.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/village-antiquarian-society-2.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/village-expedition-board.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/village-expedition-board-2.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/village-boom-club.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/village-boom-club-2.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/village-fighting-pit.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/village-fighting-pit-2.mp3

Fighting Pit arena music, MP3, looped during the actual arena run:
- assets/sounds/music/fighting-pit-arena.mp3

The "oldGuardroom" part is the dungeon theme id. Future themes should use:
- assets/sounds/music/<themeId>-exploration.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/<themeId>-combat.mp3
- assets/sounds/music/<themeId>-boss-combat.mp3

One-shot sound effects, WAV recommended:
- assets/sounds/effects/character-damage.wav
- assets/sounds/effects/enemy-defeated.wav
- assets/sounds/effects/exit-reached.wav
- assets/sounds/effects/portal.wav
- assets/sounds/effects/short-rest-finished.wav
- assets/sounds/effects/potion-drink.wav
- assets/sounds/effects/melee-attack.wav
- assets/sounds/effects/ranged-attack.wav

Recommended formats:
- Music: .mp3, 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz stereo.
- Effects: .wav for short effects, 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz mono/stereo.

Missing files are ignored by the game, so you can add them gradually.
