OLD LADY MARA — QUESTCHAIN
The Old Lady's Errands

NPC:
Old Lady Mara
Title: Keeper of the Hearth
Role: Static questgiver NPC

Theme:
A small, stubborn old woman gives practical but dangerous errands connected to the Old Guardroom. Her quests begin with gathering herbs and slowly turn into laying old soldiers, skeletons, and finally the Guardroom Commander to rest.


============================================================
QUEST 1 — GREEN VINES FOR THE POT
============================================================

Unlock:
Available immediately when Old Lady Mara is available.

Objective:
Bring 3 Green Vines.

Item requirement:
- green-vines
- or any item tagged: green-vines

Notes:
Green Vines do not need to exist yet. They are placeholder quest items you can add later.

Rewards:
- 15 gp
- 1x Old Lady's Restorative Broth
- 1x Old Lady's Hearty Soup

Reward item effects:
Old Lady's Restorative Broth:
- Consumable
- Heals 2d4 + 2 HP
- Used like a potion

Old Lady's Hearty Soup:
- Consumable
- Fully heals the hero to max HP
- Used like a potion

Flags set:
- flag.oldLady.knowsYou
- flag.oldLady.greenVinesDone

============================================================

- old-guardroom-relic

Example future items:

- broken-watch-badge

Notes:
These are placeholder relics. Later you can add small junk/treasure items with the tag old-guardroom-relic.

Reward item effect:
Old Lady's Hearth Amulet:
- Accessory
