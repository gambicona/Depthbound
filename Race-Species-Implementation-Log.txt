Race / Species Implementation Log
Updated: 2026-05-15

Overview
Race/species selection is part of hero creation and roster creation. The chosen race, subrace, Dragonborn ancestry, and Half-Elf ability choices are stored on each hero and normalized for older saves.

The spell/action system now supports a first wave of active racial powers. Features that fit the current dungeon-combat model are usable from the Abilities or Spells menus; features that need light, social play, languages, tool scenes, or condition-tagged saving throw context remain stored as traits for later systems.


Creation Flow
1. Choose save slot.
2. Choose hero name and token picture.
3. Choose race/species and subrace.
4. For Dragonborn, choose draconic ancestry.
5. For Half-Elf, choose the two +1 ability score bonuses.
6. Choose ability score method.
7. Assign ability scores.
8. Choose class spells/cantrips where applicable.
9. Choose starting gear.
10. Choose d20 luck mode.
11. Start the dungeon.

Roster hero creation at the Planning Table uses the same race/species flow.


Implemented Engine Hooks

Ability scores and movement
- Race/species ability score increases are applied to stored hero abilityScores.
- The stat assignment UI shows racial bonuses while assigning scores.
- Race speed is stored as baseSpeedFeet/speedFeet and is used by movement.
- Size is stored for Small races, though size-specific movement rules are still future work.

Damage resistances
- Race-based resistances are stored in fighter.damageResistances.
- Existing damage modifier logic reads those resistances for attacks, traps, monster specials, and spell damage.
- Status effects can now contribute temporary resistances too, used by some spell-like features.

Racial active powers
- Racial powers are injected into fighter abilities automatically from the hero's race/subrace.
- Per-rest racial spells consume their racial use, not spell points.
- Targeting, action costs, saving throws, and damage rolls reuse the normal spell system.
- Right-click cancellation works for racial spell targeting because these powers use the same targeting pipeline.

Racial cantrips
- Implemented as always-available spell entries, independent of class spell points.
- They still consume the normal action/bonus action required by the spell.

Rest handling
- Short-rest racial powers refresh on short rest and long rest.
- Long-rest racial powers refresh when the hero returns home / takes a long rest.


Implemented Races / Species

Human
- Standard Human:
  - +1 STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA
  - 30 ft walking speed


Dwarf
Base dwarf:
- +2 CON
- 25 ft walking speed
- Poison damage resistance
- Stored weapon proficiencies: Battleaxe, Handaxe, Light Hammer, Warhammer

Duergar:
- +1 STR
- Duergar Enlarge active at level 3:
  - Once per long rest
  - Action
  - Grants temporary HP and bonus weapon damage for 3 rounds
- Invisibility remains stored until stealth/visibility rules are deeper.

Hill Dwarf:
- +1 WIS
- Dwarven Toughness: +1 max HP per level

Mountain Dwarf:
- +2 STR
- Light and medium armor proficiencies stored

Not implemented yet:
- Darkvision
- Poison-save advantage
- Tool proficiencies
- Stonecunning
- Duergar sunlight sensitivity and invisibility


Elf
Base elf:
- +2 DEX
- 30 ft walking speed
- Perception proficiency stored
- Fey Ancestry stored for future charm/sleep save context

Drow:
- +1 CHA
- Stored weapon proficiencies: Rapier, Shortsword, Hand Crossbow
- Drow Faerie Fire active at level 3:
  - Once per long rest
  - Action
  - DEX save or targets are Revealed briefly
- Dancing Lights and Darkness remain stored; the light/obscurement model is not complete enough for full fidelity.

Eladrin:
- +1 INT
- Stored weapon proficiencies: Longsword, Shortsword, Shortbow, Longbow
- Fey Step active:
  - Once per short rest
  - Bonus action
  - Teleports to a visible empty square within 30 ft

High Elf:
- +1 INT
- Stored weapon proficiencies: Longsword, Shortsword, Shortbow, Longbow
- High Elf Fire Bolt cantrip active:
  - At will
  - Uses INT for the spell attack

Shadar-kai:
- +1 CON
- Necrotic damage resistance
- Blessing of the Raven Queen active:
  - Once per short rest
  - Bonus action
  - Teleports to a visible empty square within 30 ft

Wood Elf:
- +1 WIS
- 35 ft walking speed
- Stored weapon proficiencies: Longsword, Shortsword, Shortbow, Longbow
- Mask of the Wild remains stored for future stealth rules.

Not implemented yet:
- Darkvision
- Trance
- Fey Ancestry saving throw effects
- Drow sunlight sensitivity
- Full light/darkness interaction
- Wood Elf stealth benefits


Dragonborn
Base Dragonborn:
- +2 STR
- +1 CHA
- 30 ft walking speed
- Draconic ancestry selection
- Damage resistance based on ancestry damage type
- Breath Weapon active:
  - Once per short rest
  - Action
  - 15 ft cone
  - Save DC = 8 + CON modifier + proficiency bonus
  - Save type follows ancestry
  - Damage type follows ancestry
  - Damage scales: 2d6 at levels 1-5, 3d6 at 6, 4d6 at 11, 5d6 at 16
  - Combat log shows the actual damage dice roll before saves are applied

Chromatic Dragonborn:
- Black acid, Blue lightning, Green poison, Red fire, White cold resistance and breath type
- Chromatic Warding remains stored.

Gem Dragonborn:
- Amethyst force, Crystal radiant, Emerald psychic, Sapphire thunder, Topaz necrotic resistance and breath type
- Psionic Mind and Gem Flight remain stored.

Metallic Dragonborn:
- Brass fire, Bronze lightning, Copper acid, Gold fire, Silver cold resistance and breath type
- Metallic special breath effects remain stored.

Not implemented yet:
- Chromatic Warding active immunity/resistance surge
- Gem flight
- Psionic Mind
- Metallic special breath riders

Known Limitations / Future Work

No full light system yet
- Darkvision, Dancing Lights, Darkness, sunlight sensitivity, and related features are either inactive or simplified.

Limited condition-tagged saving throw context
- Fey Ancestry, Gnome Cunning, Brave, poison-save advantage, and similar traits need saving throws to know whether they are against charm, sleep, magic, poison, frightened, etc.

Limited social/tool gameplay
- Languages, tool proficiencies, social magic, disguise magic, telepathy, age, alignment, and similar features are stored or omitted until the game has scenes where they matter.

Proficiency enforcement remains soft
- Weapon and armor proficiencies are stored and displayed.
- The current equipment/attack system mostly permits use rather than blocking gear outright.

Legacy rule mix
- The project uses mostly 2014-style 5e race/species rules with some later playable options.
- Where tabletop features do not map cleanly to the current dungeon engine, they are adapted conservatively or stored for later.

2026-05-16 Addendum - New Lineages

Creation UI
- Race/species summaries now show stored traits, innate magic notes, currently usable racial abilities, racial cantrips, damage immunities, proficiencies, speed, and ability bonuses.

Aasimar (Volo version)
- Added as a race with Protector, Scourge, and Fallen subraces.
- Base: +2 CHA, 30 ft speed, necrotic/radiant resistance.
- Healing Hands active: once per long rest, action, heals self or adjacent hero for HP equal to level.
- Protector: +1 WIS, Radiant Soul active at level 3 as a radiant combat form.
- Scourge: +1 CON, Radiant Consumption active at level 3 as a stronger but riskier radiant combat form.
- Fallen: +1 STR, Necrotic Shroud active at level 3 as a close fear effect.
- Light Bearer remains stored until light matters mechanically.

Goliath (Elemental Evil version)
- Added as a race with Elemental Evil subrace.
- +2 STR, +1 CON, 30 ft speed, Athletics proficiency, cold resistance.
- Stone's Endurance active as a reaction prompt when damaged: 1d12 + CON damage reduction, once per short rest.
- Powerful Build and high-altitude acclimation are stored/noncombat.

Yuan-ti Pureblood (Volo version)
- Added as Yuan-ti race with Pureblood subrace.
- +2 CHA, +1 INT, 30 ft speed.
- Poison damage immunity implemented.
- Poison Spray cantrip active using CHA.
- Suggestion active at level 3 as a once-per-long-rest disabling command.
- Animal Friendship and full Magic Resistance are stored until beast/social and spell-save context systems are richer.

Genasi (Elemental Evil version)
- Added Genasi as one race with Air, Earth, Fire, and Water subraces.
- Base: +2 CON, 30 ft speed.
- Air: +1 DEX, Levitate active at level 3 as a defensive mobility buff. Unending Breath stored.
- Earth: +1 STR, Blade Ward cantrip active. Earth Walk and Pass without Trace stored.
- Fire: +1 INT, fire resistance, Produce Flame cantrip active, Burning Hands active at level 3.
- Water: +1 WIS, acid resistance, Acid Splash cantrip active. Amphibious and swim speed stored.
