Furniture Unique Interaction Ideas

Purpose:
This is a design pass only. Nothing here has been implemented yet.
The goal is to identify furniture that deserves a very distinct interaction instead of just "inspect for loot" or "step on hazard".

General rule:
Only make a furniture interaction unique if it changes a decision. If it is just another small loot roll, keep it generic.

Suggested interaction shape:
- One party attempt per object unless otherwise stated.
- give tooltip on what it does and what skill to use so the player can choose the best fitting hero
- Add dungeon time cost where useful: 5 minutes for quick interaction, 10 minutes for careful work, 20 minutes for complex machinery or rituals.
- Better rolls should improve the result. Example: DC success gives the basic reward, success by 5 gives bonus reward, success by 10 gives a rare reward or no drawback.
- Failure should usually cost time, trigger a hazard, make noise, or give a smaller reward instead of doing nothing.

Underused skills to lean into:
- Arcana: mana wells, crystals, portals, runes, arcane machinery.
- Religion: altars, reliquaries, fonts, idols, undead burial objects, infernal contracts.
- History: statues, obelisks, old records, sarcophagi, noble trophies.
- Insight: cursed mirrors, contracts, confession screens, strange idols.
- Performance: instruments, ritual resonance, calming echo chambers, infernal choirs.
- Persuasion / Intimidation / Deception: soul cages, contracts, cursed mirrors, sentient objects.
- Sleight of Hand: valves, locks, chain hoists, delicate traps, scroll cases.
- Animal Handling: nests, burrows, beast crates, egg sacs.
- Acrobatics: chasms, conveyor belts, unstable beams, quicksand escape.

Highest-priority unique interactions

mana-well | Mana Well
- Check: Arcana DC 13.
- Success: the using spellcaster regains spell points equal to proficiency bonus, minimum 2.
- Success by 5: regain proficiency bonus + INT/WIS/CHA spellcasting modifier, minimum 3.
- Failure: force damage or mana backlash; possibly lose 1 spell point if they have any.
- Time: 10 minutes.
- Notes: This is the cleanest "spellcaster resource fountain" object.

rune-circle | Rune Circle
- Check: Arcana or Religion DC 14.
- Success: Hero gain +1 to spell save DC or +1 to saving throws against magic until next long rest/home.
- Success by 5: apply to two party members.
- Failure: rune flare, force damage, or summons a fitting guardian.
- Time: 10 minutes.

soul-cage | Soul Cage
- Check: Persuasion, Intimidation, or Religion DC 16.
- Success: free or command a soul. Freeing gives party-wide temp HP; commanding gives one necrotic/psychic damage pulse in next combat.
- Failure: psychic damage and frightened/shaken.
- Time: 10 minutes.

contract-lectern | Contract Lectern
- Check: Insight, Religion, or Deception DC 15.
- Success: find a loophole; party gains fire resistance for one encounter or removes one curse-like penalty.
- Success by 5: also gain a small treasure/contract token.
- Failure: infernal fine print; one party member gains fire vulnerability for next encounter or loses hit points.
- Time: 10 minutes.


cursed-mirror | Cursed Mirror
- Check: Insight or Arcana DC 14.
- Success: read a future reflection; party gains advantage/bonus on next initiative or next saving throw.
- Success by 5: reveal the nearest boss/treasure room.
- Failure: psychic damage or a mirrored duplicate monster spawns.
- Time: 5 minutes.

spellbook-stand | Spellbook Stand
- Check: Arcana DC 13.
- Success: a wizard/warlock/sorcerer gains 1 spell point or a one-use scroll-like cast of a low spell.
- Success by 5: gain both.
- Failure: book snaps shut and deals force damage.
- Time: 10 minutes.

arcane-lectern | Arcane Lectern
- Check: Arcana or History DC 14.
- Success: identify the dungeon's active magical hazards, revealing traps or terrain floors in nearby rooms.
- Success by 5: disarm one visible magical trap at range.
- Failure: glyph misread; active trap pulse.
- Time: 10 minutes.

glyph-carved-pillar | Glyph-Carved Pillar
- Check: Arcana or Sleight of Hand DC 15.
- Success: rotate glyphs to mark the exit of the dungeon.
- Failure: force trap triggers.
- Time: 10 minutes.

confession-screen | Confession Screen
- Check: Insight, Persuasion, or Religion DC 13.
- Success: one party member sheds fear/guilt; remove frightened/shaken/drained if present, or gain +1 save bonus next combat.
- Failure: intrusive whispers; WIS save or shaken.
- Time: 5 minutes.

offering-bowl / offering-table / ritual-altar / altar / broken-altar
- Check: Religion DC 13, or History DC 15 for old cult context.
- Success: leave a small coin/material offering for a blessing. Blessing could be temp HP, +1 save, or undead/fiend ward.
- Success by 5: blessing affects whole party.
- Failure: improper rite; trap/hazard pulse or hostile spawn.
- Time: 10 minutes.

sacred-font
- Check: Religion or Medicine DC 13.
- Success: purify; restore HP or remove poisoned/diseased-like status.
- Success by 5: create one holy-water style consumable.
- Failure: tainted water; poison/necrotic damage.
- Time: 10 minutes.


burial-urn / broken-sarcophagus / stone-sarcophagus
- Check: Religion or History DC 14.
- Success: respectfully open; treasure without undead ambush.
- Failure: ghost/skeleton ambush or necrotic damage.
- Time: 10 minutes.


mirage-crystal
- Check: Arcana or Insight DC 14.
- Success: dispel false images; reveal hidden furniture/traps in nearby rooms.
- Success by 5: create a one-use illusion decoy.
- Failure: party suffers perception penalty until next room.
- Time: 5 minutes.


subterranean-egg-cluster / webbed-egg-sac / rot-grub-nest
- Check: Nature, Medicine, or Animal Handling DC 14.
- Success: extract rare biological material.
- Failure: poison/acid hazard or hatchling ambush.
- Time: 10 minutes.

blackwater-spring / underground-pool / bog-pool
- Check: Nature, Medicine, or Survival DC 13.
- Success: identify safe use; restore small HP, remove poison, or harvest reagent depending pool.
- Failure: poison/necrotic damage.
- Time: 5 minutes.


acid-weeping-wall / acid-pool / acid-floor
- Check: Nature, Arcana, or Alchemist-style Investigation DC 14.
- Success: neutralize/collect acid; gain acid vial or clear a few tiles.
- Failure: acid splash.
- Time: 10 minutes.

lava-floor / molten-slag / brimstone-cluster / hell-forge / forge-furnace
- Check: Arcana, Athletics, or smith-flavored Investigation DC 14.
- Success: draw heat into forge material; gain hellfire-ember, slag-glass, or temporary fire damage on next weapon hit.
- Failure: fire damage.
- Time: 10 minutes.

anvil
- Check: History, Athletics, or relevant crafting/tinker hook DC 13.
- Success: tune a weapon/armor for one dungeon: +1 damage on one weapon or +1 AC until next combat ends.
- Success by 5: no material cost.
- Failure: time loss or damaged tool/material.
- Time: 20 minutes.


incense-burner / brazier / infernal-brazier / sun-brazier
- Check: Religion, Arcana, or Medicine DC 13.
- Success: grant resistance to poison/fire/radiant depending theme.
- Failure: smoke condition or fire damage.
- Time: 5 minutes.
