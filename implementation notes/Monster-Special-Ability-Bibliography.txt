Monster Special Ability Bibliography

Scope:
This file tracks the current monster specialAbility catalogue and the runtime mechanics that currently recognize those names. The source of truth is the monster pack files in src/scripts/content/monsters and the runtime hooks in src/scripts/app/combat-map.js.

Runtime tuning:
- Monster special tuning lives in src/scripts/app/combat-map.js under monsterSpecialAbilityTuning.
- Active abilities use activeUseChance.
- On-hit riders use onHitUseChance.
- Defensive/passive-on-turn abilities use defensiveUseChance.
- A specialAbility string is only mechanical when combat-map.js recognizes it by name or by regex family.

Implemented Runtime Families

- Movement identity:
  Aquatic, BurrowAmbush, MarshAmbush, RockClimber, SewerSkulk.
  These are mostly catalogue identity abilities. The monsters express the behavior through tags, speed, flying, placement, and theme context.

- Start-of-turn self heal:
  SelfHeal.
  Once per fight, when at or below half HP, heals 1d6 + monster category HP.

- Start-of-turn blood frenzy:
  BloodFrenzy.
  If at or below half HP, gains +1 attack for that turn.

- Start-of-turn defensive brace:
  Briarhide, ShellGuard, Stubborn Beast, Thornhide, ThickHide, FrostHide, Ironbark Guard, Grove Body, Ancient Bark, Adamantine Frame, Hard Light of the Forge, Gear Assembly, Burning Guard, Furnace Shield, Mirror Heat, Cyclone Guard, Hurricane Guard, Storm Shell, Current Guard, Glacial Guard, Stone Hide, Guarding Slab, Iron Stance, Mountain Heart, Tremor Shell, Diamond Refraction, Glass Refract, Black Glass Body, Living Cover, Idol Ward, Open Sea Body, Endless Body, Vortex Body, Worldstorm Body, Firestorm Body, Molten Body.
  Defensive abilities reduce damage, cap damage, or grant a short AC/attack stance.

- Elemental self stance:
  Flare Step, Cinder Dance, Vanish into Soot, Cloudstep, Slipstream, High Roost, Whirlpool Step, Splash Step, Melt Away, Crater Step, Fault Step, Mountain Walks, Storm Eye, Sky Crown, Storm Reading, Fading Retreat, Furnace Shield, Living Cover, Open Sea Body, Endless Body, Cyclone Guard, Hurricane Guard, Glacial Guard, Current Guard, Guarding Slab, Iron Stance.
  Once per fight, the monster may gain a brief +1 AC and +1 attack elemental stance.

- Ally command aura:
  Bark Orders, Mine Lord's Edict, Stoke the Furnace, Forgeheart Pulse, Royal Furnace Oath, Heart of Ore and Flame, Command the Dead, Carrion Crown Command, Imperial Corpse Decree, Unburied Retinue, Castellan's Lockstep, Command the Coals, Pyre Command, Sultan's Decree of Flame, Pearl Command, Current of Kings, Mantle Command, Open Sky Decree, Skybreaker Law, Palace Winds, Cathedral Winds, Continental Command, Seismic Dominion.
  Nearby allies that share the relevant theme or elemental tags gain a short attack bonus.

- Active corpse barrage:
  Mass Grave Mortar, Corpse Cart Spill.
  Once per fight, targets up to two visible heroes at range. Targets make a DEX save, then take bludgeoning or poison damage, with failed saves also causing a short attack penalty.

- On-hit venom/poison:
  Claw Fever, Deep Venom, Infernal Sting, Rotting Tendrils, Sickening Claws, Venom Bite, VenomBite, Venom Needle, Wyrmwood Sap.
  Target makes a CON save. Failed saves take poison damage. Sickening/sap variants also apply a small attack penalty.

- On-hit smoke, ash, choking, and airless pressure:
  Ash Cough, Choking Grasp, Dead Sky, Drowning Mist, Drying Wind, Dust Bite, Dust Cough, Sandblind Ambush, Silence of No Air, Smoke, Smoke Veil, Suffocating Rain, Thin Air Aura, Mist Choke, Airless Bite.
  Target makes a CON save. Failed saves suffer short attack and/or movement penalties.

- On-hit fire and heat:
  Burning Guard, Cinder Crown, Heated Spear, Ignite Ground, Kindle, Lava Wake, Magma Breath, Melt Armor, Scalding Puff, White-Hot Beam, Boiling Spray.
  Target makes a DEX save. Failed saves take fire damage. Armor/beam variants may apply a short AC penalty.

- On-hit cutting shards and glass:
  Aurora Slash, Bleeding Edge, Glass Splinters, Glasswind Cut, Hailglass Volley, Needle Draft, Needle Spray, Prismatic Lance, Razor Pass, Salt Lash, Shard Pin, Shatter Spines.
  Target makes a DEX save. Failed saves take slashing damage. Pinning shard variants slow movement.

- On-hit lightning and thunder:
  Chain Coil, Choir Blast, Crackling Pulse, Lesser Stormcall, Lightning Lash, Queenly Thunderbolt, Rod Draw, Static Bite, Stormlord Descent, Thunderclap.
  Target makes a DEX or STR save. Failed saves take lightning or thunder damage and may lose reaction or be pulled.

- On-hit life drain and soul drain:
  Abyssal Drain, Astral Reap, Devour Soul, Greater Life Drain, Life Drain, Unending Appetite.
  Target makes a CON save. Failed saves take necrotic damage, get a short drained movement penalty, and some variants heal the monster.

- On-hit fear/curse/malice:
  Bone Debt, Condemning Mark, Dread Whisper, Marrow Verdict, Mind-Pounce, Mindrot Sermon, Needling Malice, Ruin Hymn, Ruin Scratch, Mindrot Cloud, Corruptive Sporulation.
  Target makes a WIS save. Failed saves suffer short attack/save penalties or lose reaction.

- On-hit bloodied finisher:
  Acid Maw, Bite and Tear, Blood in the Water, Blood Scent, Devouring Bloom, Engulfing Mass, Feeding Frenzy, Flesh Verdict, Salt the Wound, Thousand Maws.
  If the target is at or below half HP, the monster deals extra damage. Some variants heal from the damage.

- Pull, chain, and drag riders:
  Baronial Undertow, Bone Cage, Burial Pull, Canopy Snatch, Chain Coil, Dragged into the Teeth, Dragging Lash, Drop the Hook, Hook and Drag, Hookcap Pull, Hooking Chain, Leviathan Drag, Living Chains, Luring Scent, Pull Under, Spectral Chain, Tidal Pull.
  Target makes a STR save. Failed saves may be pulled 5 ft and sometimes restrained.

- Restraint and binding riders:
  Bone Cage, Cinder Chain Judgment, Clay Bind, Constricting Coil, Coral Snare, Crushing Claws, Engulfing Slide, Forest Judgment, Heated Coil, Hoist Prisoner, Living Chain, Mud Grip, Root Lock, Stranglehold.
  Target makes a STR save. Failed saves are briefly restrained.

- Hamstring, web, and cold slow:
  Cold Bite, Crippling Bite, Dragging Grasp, Drowning Grip, Frozen Undertow, Gear Nip, Glacial Advance, Grinding Teeth, Hamstring Bite, Ice Slick, Polar Night, Sleep Poison Bolt, Web Snare, WebSnare.
  Target makes a STR or DEX save. Failed saves reduce or stop movement briefly.

- Charge, pounce, slam, rush, and quake hit:
  Avalanche Hammer, Briar Slam, Burning Dive, Charge, Corpse Slam, Crater Slam, Crushing Deep, Crushing Stomp, Falling Fronds, Falling Star Dive, Faultline Strike, Four-Season Slam, Impaling Advance, Lava Step, Magma Fault, Mauling Rush, Minecart Shove, Mudslide Rush, Pillar Fall, Pounce, Pouncing Vines, Predator Lunge, Pressure Crush, Quake Fist, Rift Charge, Shattering Charge, Siege Charge, Stomp, Stone Pounce, Support-Beam Breaker, Swooping Strike, Thunder Charge, Thunderhead Crash, Tyrant Downburst, Whitewater Rush, World-Stamp.
  If the monster moved far enough this turn, the hit adds extra damage and can shove or knock prone on a failed STR save.

- Active fire/forge bursts:
  Ashen Burst, Blinding Cyclone, Caldera Gate, Coal Toss, Dead Sky, Ember Mortar, Eruption Cycle, Fireball, Furnace Vent, Hellhound Breath, Lava Breath, Magma Breath, Molten Slag Breath, Open the Pyre, Split the Battlefield, Suffocating Rain, Volcanic Pulse, World-Pyre Ascension.
  Area special. Targets save, take fire damage, and may be scorched or pushed.

- Active storm/air bursts:
  Baronial Cyclone, Breath of the Plane, Cathedral Winds, Choir Blast, City-Eater Winds, Crackling Pulse, Dust Spin, Neverending Storm, Pressure Rift, Queenly Thunderbolt, Regent Stormfall, Split the Heavens, Starstorm Fall, Tempest Choir, Thunderclap, Tyrant Downburst, Worldstorm Body.
  Area special. Targets save, take thunder/lightning damage, and may be pushed or deafened.

- Active earth bursts:
  Avalanche Hammer, Collapse District, Crown of Cairns, Fault Throne, Graveyard Slam, Needle Spray, Open Sinkhole, Pillar Fall, Quake Fist, Royal Tremor, Seismic Sentence, Sovereign Faultline, Tectonic Verdict.
  Area special. Targets save, take bludgeoning or slashing damage, and may be pushed or shaken.

- Active tide/water bursts:
  Abyssal Pressure, Boiling Spray, Cloudburst Devour, Corpse Tide, Crown Tide, Crush of Oceans, Crushing Wave, Drown the World, Endless Deluge, Glacial Advance, Hailglass Volley, Iceberg Break, Leviathan Roll, Tsunami Front, Worldspring Eruption.
  Area special. Targets save, take bludgeoning/cold/fire damage, and may be pushed, pulled, or slowed.

- Active plague/spore/poison bursts:
  Abyssal Bile, Bile Spray, Blight Belch, Carrion Spores, Midnight Spores, Plague Breath, Plague King's Mass, Rot Burst, Rot Crown Pulse, Spores of Filth, Titan Sporefall, Venom Bloom, Vomit Plague.
  Area special. Targets save, take poison or acid damage, and may be poisoned/scorched.

- Active dread/psychic/shout bursts:
  Abyss Unleashed, Abyssal Roar, Banshee Keening, Cathedral Dirge, Chaos Star, Crown of the Ninefold Pact, Doom Scream, Duke's War Cry, Grief Pulse, Hollow Wail, Horror Judgement, Howl of Hunger, Mournful Cry, Origin Wail, Overmind Spores, Panic Shriek, Panic Spores, Royal Wail, Shriek Alarm, Triple Condemnation, Void Bell Toll, White Bell Wail.
  Area special. Targets save, take psychic/thunder damage, and may be frightened.

- Active soul/necrotic bursts:
  Corrupt Wish, Forbidden Chorus, Grave Breath, Moonlit Dominion, Soul Furnace, Soul Lantern.
  Area special. Targets save, take necrotic damage, and may be drained.

- Active force/abyssal/rift bursts:
  Flaming Whipstorm, Gate Pulse, Maw of the Abyss, Reality Tear, Rift Sovereignty, Soul Tempest, Abyss Storm, Unstable Fire.
  Area special. Targets save, take force/fire/lightning/piercing damage and may be pushed or pulled.

- Active compulsion/debuff:
  Chains of Grace, False Lantern, False Promise, Infernal Verdict, Lesser Possession, Luring Scent, Musk Charm, Name in the Ledger, Noble Possession, Petty Bargain, Praetor's Challenge, Prince's Mark, Sulphur Hex, Sweetened Damnation.
  Status special. Target saves or suffers a short attack/AC debuff, sometimes with pull.

- Active binding/chains:
  Chains of the Ninth Gate, Command the Brambles, Constricting Coil, Forest Judgment, Living Chains, Living Jungle, Locking Chain, Root Snare, Sentence to Chains, Stranglehold.
  Status special. Targets save or are briefly restrained and may be pulled.

- Reactive body/riposte:
  Abyssal Riposte, Barbed Hide, Black Glass Body, Blackwater Seep, Cinder Body, Cinder Crown, Crown of Thorns, Eidolic Reversal, Firestorm Body, Ghostly Riposte, Molten Body, Open Sea Body, Rooted Counter, Shard Burst, Shatter Spines, Storm Shell, Thorn Hide, Thorn Nest, Tremor Shell, Vortex Body, Worldstorm Body.
  Melee attackers may take reactive damage. Some variants push the attacker.

- Death defiance:
  Black Rebirth, Endless Body, Endless Gale, Final Bargain, Fungal Rebirth, Hollow Choir Rebuild, Infernal Wishflame, King's Return, Mass Grave Rebuild, Mountain Heart, Older Than Roads, Prince's Second Form, Primordial Regrowth, Unfinished Death, Absolute Stillness.
  One-time survival at 1 HP, subject to damage-type exceptions for some older families.

- Death burst:
  Continental Melt, Death Throes, End-Breath Ash, Greater Death Throes, Iceberg Break, Industrial Catastrophe, Pebble Scatter, Split the Heavens, Worldspring Eruption.
  When the monster dies, nearby heroes save or take area damage.

- Terrain-like floor specials:
  Storm Current Floor, Tidal Current Floor, Unstable Stone Floor.
  These are furniture/terrain abilities, not monster specialAbility entries. They apply hazard/difficult terrain and may shove characters on failed saves.
