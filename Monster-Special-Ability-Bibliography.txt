Monster Special Ability Bibliography

Tuning lives in src/scripts/app.js under monsterSpecialAbilityTuning.
Active abilities use activeUseChance. On-hit riders use onHitUseChance. Defensive/passive-on-turn abilities use defensiveUseChance.

Implemented mechanical families:

- Aquatic, RockClimber, SewerSkulk
  Biome/movement identity abilities. Currently descriptive; these monsters already express their mobility through speed and placement.

- BloodFrenzy
  If the monster is at or below half HP at the start of its turn, it gains +1 attack for that turn.

- BossRoar, Root-Rending Roar, Stormhorn Burst, Gravequake, Stampede
  Active area ability. Nearby heroes make a Strength save. Failed saves take bludgeoning damage and suffer the Shaken attack penalty until the end of their next turn.

- Briarhide, ShellGuard, Stubborn Beast, Thornhide
  Defensive start-of-turn ability. Once per fight, the monster may gain +2 AC until the end of its turn.

- Bile Spray, Blight Belch, Plague Breath, Rot Burst, Rot Crown Pulse
  Active area ability. Nearby heroes make a Constitution save. Failed saves take poison damage; successful saves take half.

- Charge, Corpse Slam, Crushing Stomp, Mauling Rush, Pounce, Predator Lunge, Swooping Strike, Thunder Charge
  On-hit rider after the monster moved at least 20 ft this turn. Adds bludgeoning damage and may shove the target 5 ft on a failed Strength save.

- Claw Fever, Deep Venom, Sickening Claws, Venom Bite, VenomBite
  On-hit venom rider. Target makes a Constitution save. Failed saves take poison damage. Sickening variants also apply a small attack penalty.

- Crippling Bite, Dragging Grasp, Drowning Grip, Hamstring Bite, Web Snare, WebSnare
  On-hit restraint rider. Target makes a Strength or Dexterity save. Failed saves have movement stopped until the end of their next turn.

- Fireball, Lesser Fireball
  Active ranged area ability. Visible heroes in range make a Dexterity save. Failed saves take fire damage; successful saves take half.

- Grave Spark, Venom Spit
  Active ranged special shot. Targeted heroes make a Dexterity save. Failed saves take necrotic or poison damage; successful saves take half.

- SelfHeal
  Defensive start-of-turn ability. Once per fight, when at or below half HP, the monster may heal 1d6 + category HP.

- ThickHide, FrostHide
  Passive durability identity abilities. The actual resistance is read from the monster's damageResistances field.

- Undead Fortitude
  Defensive death trigger. When reduced to 0 HP, the monster may make a Constitution save against DC 5 + incoming damage. On success it remains at 1 HP.

- Example Ability
  Template-only placeholder. No gameplay effect.
