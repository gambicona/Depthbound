Plans:

- different attack types: weapon and spell, melee and ranged, each with ranges and damage types (non-magical/magical and all normal dnd damage types)
- different monster types and behaviour (melee as we have, ranged like archer, ranged like mage, tank like)
- progression: each monster awards xp
- stats as in dnd for PCs and monsters (str dex con wis int cha)
- simple level up (higher hp, stat increase, prof increase)
- loot system (gold, weapons, armor, healing potions, scrolls etc)
- new classes as PCs unlockable by finding in the dungeon, from home you can choose which character goes exploring
- multiple characters form a party to go explore
- multiple monsters depending on CR and number PCs
- traps and skill challenges
- Characters typically have two defaults attacks, one melee and ond ranged. Not all have ranged. then they have special abilities (like fighter has one more attack, cleric can heal, mages fireball etc to give class fluff)
- bosses have special abilities
- selectable dungeon difficulty (10% more HP, extra abilities and such)
- different types of movement (walking, flying, teleport)


Different from "real" dnd:
- spells are simplified
- skill are simplified
- anything to related to combat or exploration utility gets cut down
- new balancement of classes when compared to DnD
